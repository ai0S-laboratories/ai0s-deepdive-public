### "Replicating Evolution = `Staging`": The Difference Compared to Biology

In biological nature, there is no `Staging`: every trial takes place directly on the living organism in real time. If a path fails (`0`), it costs real lifetime or survival.

In the digital world, the harness can provide test environments; their isolation, costs and correspondence to reality must each be checked:
* **Replication alone is not evolution** (mere `1:1` copying spins in place).
* Suitable **`Staging`** can examine variants and failures before a live system is changed. Isolation and rollback require verification; tests cannot cover every real-world outcome. Results and limitations, together with discarded paths, reach the Review Point (`PS`) before humans decide on implementation.
